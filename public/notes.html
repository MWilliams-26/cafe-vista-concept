Café Vista Refresh brief View the new site

Prepared for Balsayat Jorayeva · November 2026

Your café is better than your website.

Café Vista has a 4.6 on Google from 270+ reviews, press from Hoboken Girl, and photography most cafés would envy. Online, though, a few broken links quietly send customers to the wrong place. Here is what we found, and what the new site fixes.

What’s costing you customers today

  1. “Order Online” sends Hoboken customers to Ridgefield Park.

    The DoorDash and Grubhub buttons on cafevistahoboken.com open the 192 Main St, Ridgefield Park listings, and “Call us” shows Ridgefield Park’s number, (201) 853-9444. A Hoboken customer ordering from the website gets a café in another county. Your Hoboken Grubhub and DoorDash listings exist; the site just doesn’t link to them.

  2. Yelp’s “Website” and “Menu” links open a gambling site.

    Yelp and MapQuest still list your old address, cafevista-hoboken.com. That domain has lapsed and now redirects to an Indonesian online gambling site (we checked on November 5, 2026). Anyone tapping “Full menu” from your Yelp page lands there.

  3. Your hours are different everywhere.

    Google says 7:30–4 on weekdays and 7:30–5 on weekends. Yelp and Apple Maps say 8–5 every day. Your Instagram bio says 7:30–5:30. Grubhub says 8–4. Your own website doesn’t list hours at all. People who arrive to a closed door don’t come back.

  4. The menu is a photo, and the events are a flyer.

    Menus and event packages are single images. Google can’t read them, they’re hard to zoom on a phone, and nobody can find “Turkish coffee Hoboken” or “high tea party Hoboken” on your site. The menu prices also differ from your Grubhub listing.

  5. Search engines see an empty page.

    The site loads everything with JavaScript, with no description, address or hours in the page itself, so Google has little to show. The Locations page describes Ridgefield Park and Jersey City but not Hoboken, Gift Cards and Catering say “Coming soon,” the footer’s Gift Cards link is misspelled, and the Grubhub logo is a broken image.

What the new site does about it

  • Order in one tap. Hoboken’s own Grubhub, DoorDash and phone number, in the header, the menu and a sticky bar on phones.
  • Open or closed, at a glance. A live “Open now · until 4 pm” sign and one hours table, written so Google can read it.
  • A real menu. Every drink and dish as text, easy to read on a phone and easy for Google to index.
  • Sells your after-hours space. The High Tea, Kids’ Tea and Rent-the-Café packages get their own section and an inquiry form that emails you a ready-to-answer request. Evening events earn money while the café would otherwise be closed.
  • Tells your story. Your Istanbul roots, the vintage china and your Sufi motto, which is exactly what reviewers love: “served in fine china, which makes me feel valued.”
  • Built for search. Structured business data (address, hours, menu, cuisine), a proper title and description, and fast-loading images.

Quick wins you can do this week

  • On Yelp, change the website to cafevistahoboken.com and remove the old menu link.
  • Pick one set of hours and use it on Google, Yelp, Apple Maps, Instagram and Grubhub.
  • Ask your web person to point “Order Online” at the Hoboken listings today, even before a redesign.
  • Add the event packages and photos to your Google Business Profile.

Before launch, please confirm

  • Current hours for each day.
  • Menu items and prices (we used the menu on your current site).
  • Which photos were taken at the Hoboken café, and the photographer’s name for credit.
  • That the photo of the founder is you, and that you’re happy with the story and the quote.
  • Permission to quote the three Google reviews shown.
  • Event package prices and rules, and the email address that should receive inquiries.
  • Whether there’s an Uber Eats listing for Hoboken.
