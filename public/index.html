Our story

A café curated like a home.

“I’ve always enjoyed curating spaces, hosting guests, and creating an experience for them,” says Balsayat, who grew up in Istanbul. When she opened Café Vista in the summer of 2020, as a new mom, in the middle of a pandemic, she filled it with the things she loves: serveware hunted down in vintage shops, fresh flowers in teacups, a hand-painted mural and a record playing in the background.

The china lives in an armoire by the door. Every cup is a little different, and that’s the point.

“Do not rest until you turn your dreams into moments.”

The Sufi saying Balsayat lives by

Visit the family: Dream Vista in Jersey City, with its garden courtyard, and Café Vista Ridgefield Park.
